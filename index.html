nf-pangenome-elaeis

Undergraduate thesis, 2026

A Nextflow pipeline that builds a pangenome graph from three oil palm (Elaeis guineensis) genome assemblies with Minigraph-Cactus. It runs on BRIN's Mahameru HPC cluster under SLURM, and I benchmarked CPU and memory use to recommend resource settings from real runs instead of guessing high.

Built with Nextflow DSL2, Minigraph-Cactus, Python, Bash and SLURM

About

I'm Doni, an Information Systems student at Universitas Riau. I like owning a project end to end: the screens people use, the backend that keeps the data straight, and lately the pipelines that process genomes.

My thesis automates oil palm pangenome construction on BRIN's Mahameru HPC cluster. Before that I built a data platform during my internship at Batam's social services office, and right now I'm writing the backend for a three-branch bookstore with my team.

Off the keyboard it's anime and chess, which is why both keep showing up in my projects.
